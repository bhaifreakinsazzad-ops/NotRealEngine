# Website service scope reconciliation

This records historical working definitions without presenting them as newly approved final commercial terms.

| Offer | Preserved working definition | Commercial status |
|---|---|---|
| Single Page Website Development | One page, maximum five approved content sections | Quote required. BDT 999 promotion is recorded but not checkout eligible. |
| Mini Website Development | One page, maximum eight approved content sections | Quote required. The prototype's “3–5 page” wording is rejected. |
| Business Website Development | Multi-page business website | Quote required. Exact page count/package requires approval. |

Forms, bilingual content, analytics, editable content, blogs, and other capabilities are not silently reclassified as universally included or paid. They are represented as included foundations or scoped options according to the recorded working catalog, and any final package/pricing decision requires commercial approval metadata.
